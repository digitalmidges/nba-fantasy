Best bang for the buck

Who returned the most fantasy points for where they were drafted.

Value = overall pick # minus production rank. Production rank orders all drafted players who have played by the average of their FPts-total rank and FPts/G rank (1 = best). +40 means a player is producing about 40 spots better than his draft slot. STEAL = round 4 or later and a top-20 producer, or value +40 and up. BUST = rounds 1–3 and value −25 or worse. Early season: most players have only 2–3 games, so expect big swings.
